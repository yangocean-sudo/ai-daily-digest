## AI Summary

OpenClaw Control Center provides a visual interface for monitoring and controlling OpenClaw agents locally. It addresses the "black box" concern with AI agents by offering transparency into agent operations, allowing users to see what agents are doing, verify actions, and maintain control. This is particularly valuable for developers and teams requiring oversight of autonomous AI systems.
