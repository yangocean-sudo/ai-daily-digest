## Description

AI Hedge Fund is a sophisticated multi-agent system that replicates the structure and functions of a real hedge fund team using AI agents. Different agents take on specialized roles (analysts, portfolio managers, risk assessors) to collaboratively analyze investments and make trading decisions. It's both an educational tool and a potential foundation for algorithmic trading systems.

## Key Features

- Specialized AI agents for different financial roles
- Investment analysis capabilities
