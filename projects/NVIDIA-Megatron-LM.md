## AI Summary

Megatron-LM is NVIDIA's flagship framework for training large language models at massive scale. Developed by NVIDIA's research team, it powers many of the biggest AI models in production today.

Key capabilities:
- **Model parallelism**: Efficiently distribute models across thousands of GPUs
- **Pipeline parallelism**: Overlapping computation and communication
- **Tensor parallelism**: Split individual layers across devices
- **Sequence parallelism**: Handle extremely long contexts
- **Mixed precision training**: FP16/BF16 for faster training with minimal accuracy loss

Megatron-LM has been used to train models with hundreds of billions to trillions of parameters. It's the backbone of NVIDIA's AI offerings and has been adopted by major AI labs and cloud providers for their largest training runs.

The framework continues to evolve with the latest advances in distributed training and efficiency optimizations.
