## AI Summary

AgentScope is a multi-agent orchestration platform focused on **transparency and observability**. Unlike traditional "black box" AI agent frameworks, AgentScope provides:

- **Visual debugging**: See exactly what each agent is thinking and doing at every step
- **Structured logging**: Machine-readable logs for production monitoring
- **Interpretable pipelines**: Clear decision traces so you can understand why an agent made a choice
- **Multi-agent coordination**: Built-in support for complex agent workflows and handoffs

It's particularly designed for production AI systems where trust and debuggability matter more than raw capability. Think of it as the "DevTools for AI agents" — bringing software engineering best practices to the LLM era.
