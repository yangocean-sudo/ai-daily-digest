## Description

Airi is a self-hosted AI companion platform that brings virtual characters to life. Capable of realtime voice chat, playing Minecraft, Factorio, and more. Supports Web, macOS, and Windows platforms.

## Key Features

- Realtime voice chat capabilities
- Gaming integration (Minecraft, Factorio)
- Self-hosted for privacy and control
- Multi-platform support (Web, macOS, Windows)
- Character/personality customization

## AI Summary

Airi represents the evolution of AI companions from simple chatbots to immersive, interactive entities.
