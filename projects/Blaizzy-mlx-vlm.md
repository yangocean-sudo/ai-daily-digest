MLX-VLM is a package for inference and fine-tuning of Vision Language Models (VLMs) on your Mac using MLX.

## AI Summary

MLX-VLM brings state-of-the-art Vision Language Models to Apple Silicon Macs, enabling local inference and fine-tuning using Apple's MLX framework. This is significant for developers who want to run multimodal AI models locally without relying on cloud services.
